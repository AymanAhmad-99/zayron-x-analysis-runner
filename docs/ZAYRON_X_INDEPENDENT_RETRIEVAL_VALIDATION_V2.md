## 4. FORMAL BOUNDARY SPECIFICATION

To maintain scientific integrity and prevent ungrounded extrapolation, the findings are categorized into four mandatory, non-overlapping domains:

### A. WHAT WAS PROVEN
1. **Cloudflare Vectorize + D1 Integration**: Real vector similarity searches execute against `zayron-knowledge-index` and resolve verbatim text chunks via D1 `knowledge_chunks` with 100% resolution on tested sets.
2. **Cryptographic Provenance**: Resolved chunks carry immutable SHA-256 hashes of verbatim text alongside complete metadata (`query_id`, `retrieval_id`, `vector_id`, `document_id`, `authority_tier`).
3. **Precondition Gating**: Non-negotiable domain preconditions prevent cross-domain contamination. Pure vulnerability signals cannot claim *Art of Memory Forensics*; host-only events cannot cite packet-capture methodology.
4. **Counterfactual Causality**: Excising a knowledge chunk (Run B) measurably alters the analytical depth and confidence of the report, while substituting an irrelevant chunk (Run C) is deterministically rejected.
5. **Zero Public Production Alteration**: The entire validation was conducted strictly within shadow isolated paths (`workers/api` shadow endpoint and offline validation suite). Public ingestion routes remain untouched.

### B. WHAT WAS OBSERVED
1. **D1 API Remote Rate Limits**: Wrangler CLI queries to D1 experienced rate limits when queried heavily from external non-worker environments; internal worker bindings (`env.DB`) and batch-buffered pipelines bypass this ceiling.
2. **Semantic Similarity Clustering**: CVE technical descriptions and threat actor advisories consistently cluster at cosine similarities between 0.78 and 0.94 against methodology chunks.
3. **Corpus Coverage**: 25,642 active vectors cover core books and MITRE ATT&CK techniques, with 11,321 staged chunks awaiting future indexing batches.

### C. WHAT WAS NOT TESTED
1. **Multi-Million Vector Scale**: The system was validated against the 25,642 active vectors in `zayron-knowledge-index`, not a multi-million vector enterprise index.
2. **Live Zero-Day Streaming at High Concurrency**: Concurrency stress testing beyond 50 simultaneous parallel Vectorize calls was not performed in this round.
3. **Multi-Language Knowledge Corpora**: Knowledge corpus chunks are currently in English; foreign-language intelligence signals are translated during normalization before vector retrieval.

### D. WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN
1. **Optimal Top-K for Complex Multi-CVE Exploits**: Whether Top-K=5 is sufficient when a campaign chains 4 distinct CVEs across firmware, web gateway, and kernel remains to be evaluated in Phase 3.
2. **Long-Term Drift of BGE-Base Embeddings**: Model embedding drift across multiple years of evolving threat slang and terminology updates requires continuous re-benchmarking.
