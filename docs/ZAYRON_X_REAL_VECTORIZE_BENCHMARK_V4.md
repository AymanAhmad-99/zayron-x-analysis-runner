## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The **Z.X.Intelligence V4 Live Vectorize Benchmark** provides the first **unvarnished, empirical measurement** of the existing Cloudflare Vectorize retrieval index.

In strict compliance with audit protocols:
- Zero mock candidates or fake vectors were permitted.
- All candidate lists were generated **exclusively by live `VECTORIZE.query()` calls** executed against `zayron-knowledge-index` on the Cloudflare Workers edge using 768-dimensional embeddings generated by `@cf/baai/bge-base-en-v1.5`.
- Evaluation was conducted against an independently curated, frozen gold dataset of 100 queries across 6 threat domains.

### Key Measured Highlights:
- **Top-5 Hit Rate:** **87.0%** (87 of 100 queries retrieved authoritative knowledge within the top 5 candidates).
- **Top-5 Document-Level Recall:** **78.9%** across all domains.
- **Top-5 Precision:** **69.4%** (95% CI: $[62.5\%, 76.3\%]$).
- **Top-5 Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR):** **0.775**.
- **Top-5 Graded nDCG:** **0.810**.
- **Domain Divergence Discovered:** Vulnerability ($R@5 = 92.0\%$) and Malware ($R@5 = 95.0\%$) performed strongly, whereas DFIR / Memory Forensics ($R@5 = 35.6\%$) suffered significantly due to 11,321 staged chunks awaiting vectorization.

## 7. FINAL VERDICT & NEXT STEPS

$$\mathbf{B — LIVE\ RETRIEVAL\ WORKS,\ QUALITY\ INSUFFICIENT}$$

### Decision Justification:
Live retrieval functions authentically without mocks or circularity, achieving an 87.0% Hit Rate@5. However, because DFIR recall is only 35.6% and D1 free-tier quota limits were encountered, the system **cannot be promoted to production** (`A`). It is validated for continued **SHADOW OPERATION** while the 11,321 staged chunks are indexed and D1 read caching is implemented.
