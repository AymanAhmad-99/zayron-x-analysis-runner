# ZAYRON-X Telegram Policy

## Protect
- Telegram authentication
- Telegram session
- Durable Object
- official publication destination
- collector state
- publish mechanism

## Rules
- Do not create duplicate collectors.
- Do not regenerate production sessions casually.
- Do not move Telegram runtime to external infrastructure unless authorized.
- Do not replace the official publication channel.
- Do not mix source collection with outbound publication.
