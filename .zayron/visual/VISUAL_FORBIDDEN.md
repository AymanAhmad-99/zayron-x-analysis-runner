# ZAYRON-X — VISUAL FORBIDDEN LIST

**Authority:** Master Visual Source of Truth  
**Enforcement:** Automated CI Visual Linting & Review Gate  

---

## The Non-Negotiable Prohibitions

Any pull request or AI generation containing the following visual elements will be **BLOCKED**:

1. ❌ **Cyberpunk / Neon Aesthetics**: No glowing green matrix text, bright cyan neon glows, laser grids, or "hacker movie" tropes.
2. ❌ **Glassmorphism & Excessive Backdrop Blur**: No transparent milky frosted-glass panels (`backdrop-blur-xl`, semi-transparent white fills). Surfaces must be solid, crisp, and architecturally bounded.
3. ❌ **Gaudy Gradients**: No purple-to-pink or multi-stop rainbow gradients. Backgrounds must be flat warm neutrals (`#F5F4F0`) or solid white (`#FFFFFF`) / dark (`#111215`).
4. ❌ **3D Rotating Globes & Particle Canvases**: No CPU-heavy spinning 3D wireframe spheres, particle physics networks, or canvas starfields.
5. ❌ **Pill & Capsule Overload**: Buttons and cards must use restrained, subtle rounding (`4px`–`8px`). Do NOT make every container a giant 9999px rounded capsule.
6. ❌ **Fake "Stock Dashboard" Gimmicks**: No arbitrary wave graphs with no axis labels, fake CPU/RAM dials, or animated speedometer gauges.
7. ❌ **Decorative Terminal / CRT Interfaces**: No fake terminal windows with green scanlines (`> root@zayron:~$ ping darkweb`). ZAYRON-X is a modern institutional intelligence platform, not a retro command prompt.
8. ❌ **Unrestrained Signal Color**: Orange (`#D9531E`) is an **alert signal**, not a decorative background fill or primary button color. It must never dominate page layouts.
9. ❌ **Fake Enterprise Logos & Fictitious Customer Testimonials**: No fabricated Fortune 500 logos or fake quotes.
10. ❌ **Unstructured Visual Chaos**: No non-standard fonts, ad-hoc inline hex colors, or random layout widths that break the 1280px max-width container.
