# ZAYRON-X — MASTER VISUAL SOURCE OF TRUTH

**Classification:** Architectural Design Constitution  
**Permanent Authority:** Non-Negotiable Reference  

---

## 1. Declarative Mandate

The approved ZAYRON-X design specification established in the Master Visual Reference is the **Single Authoritative Visual Source of Truth** for all current and future interfaces across the platform.

Future engineers and AI agents must **never** invent ad-hoc styles, novel color palettes, or disconnected UI paradigms.

---

## 2. What Must Be Globally Preserved

1. **Typography Character**:
   - Master Serif Display headlines for high-impact section titles (`UNDERSTAND WHAT CHANGED.`, `INTELLIGENCE, NOT NOISE`).
   - Clean, mathematical Inter / Geist Sans for telemetry, metadata, and body typography.
2. **Color Balance & Restraint**:
   - Predominance of warm neutral canvas (`#F5F4F0`) and solid white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`).
   - Dark graphite sections (`#111215`) reserved for method pipelines and action frameworks.
   - Signal orange (`#D9531E`) restricted purely to live telemetry pulses, active indicators, and high severity badges.
3. **Chapter-Based Information Architecture**:
   - Clear chapter identifiers (`CHAPTER 01 - IDENTITY`, `CHAPTER 06 - INTELLIGENCE`) providing structured reading orientation.
4. **Institutional Restraint**:
   - Clean 1px architectural borders (`#E1DFD7`).
   - Precise 4px–8px border radii.
   - Zero gratuitous animations, neon effects, or decorative clutter.

---

## 3. What May Evolve Per Route

1. **Information Density**: Dossier and investigation routes may feature higher data density, split-pane tables, and deep entity correlation graphs.
2. **Data Visualizations**: Specific threat heatmaps, MITRE ATT&CK matrices, and timeline charts tailored to the domain requirements.
3. **Interactive Filtering**: Real-time facet selection, multi-indicator query builders, and IOC export desks.

---

## 4. Design Evolution Protocol

Any modification to the visual system must adhere to `.zayron/visual/DESIGN_CHANGE_POLICY.md` and pass the automated `.zayron/scripts/visual-governance-check.cjs` before merge.
